## 1. Questions

- **N1: learned vs DA, S0.** Given the same observations, where do these three
  schemes sit relative to ETKF / EnKF / EnKS / 4D-Var (S0 forced: 0.774 / 0.761 /
  0.812 / 0.840, mean of the four EVs)?
  - a deterministic network (DirectUNet);
  - an obs-conditioned flow (CFM);
  - a learned prior with observation guidance (SDA).
- **N2: model error.** S1 is now model error only: wind, rd, drag and DA
  resolution (§2.3). The observations are identical in S0 and S1.
  - **For the obs-only networks, S1 = S0 by construction.** Same truth, same
    observations, no DA model, so the same output.
  - Each network is therefore evaluated once, and the number appears in both
    columns, marked as identical by construction.
  - The question N2 answers is how far DA falls under model error, relative to
    a scheme that never uses the model. The comparison is with the re-run S1
    and S1-tuned DA rows.
- **N4: uncertainty.** For the ensembles (CFM, SDA): CRPS and spread/RMSE next to
  the DA ensembles' columns.
- **N5: forced → coupled.** Train on the forced dataset only and test on both test
  sets. Given the wind the ocean physics are identical, and the obs-only networks
  never see the wind, so the scores should agree within intervals.
- *(Round 2, N3: do the wind / parameter conditioning and the lagged initial
  state help? Out of round 1, §8.)*

| scoring | the DA driver's `window_metrics` on the **2-hourly truth** (§2.2): per-window EV of ψ₁, ψ₂, q₁, q₂ with the window's own parameters; score = mean of the four; bootstrap 95% intervals over windows; ensembles add CRPS and spread/RMSE |

### 2.1 Training target: daily means (checked in the code)

- `QGNeuralDataset.__getitem__` (`data/qg_neural.py`) builds:
  - the ψ target from `psi_daily`, the inversion of the 12-step mean of the PV
    state, z-scored per layer;
  - the q target from `q_daily` (`states_q`, raw units).
- **Both loss terms are therefore against daily means.**
- The q term (`_q_loss`, `train_qg_neural.py`) de-normalizes the predicted ψ,
  inverts it to PV with the window's own rd, and takes the MSE against
  `q_daily`.
- The inversion is linear, so the daily mean of ψ equals ψ of the daily-mean q
  exactly; the two targets are consistent.
- Each column is observed once per day, at one random 2-hourly step. A daily bin
  therefore holds an *instantaneous* value, while the target is the daily mean.
  This is the legacy convention and is kept; the network learns that mapping.

### 2.2 Evaluation: interpolation to 2 hours

- The network outputs 30 daily means. They are placed at the day centres,
  t_d = (d + ½) days, for d = 0 … 29.
- **Cubic spline in time** (not-a-knot), independently per grid point and layer,
  evaluated at the 360 two-hourly steps of the truth.
  - The first and last half-days (6 steps each) are extrapolated by the end
    cubic pieces.
  - These steps are flagged in the per-day error curve.
- Interpolation is done on ψ. q at 2 hours is then the per-step inversion with
  the window's rd. Interpolation and inversion are both linear, so this equals
  interpolating q.
- **Ensembles:** each member is interpolated, then the ensemble mean, CRPS and
  spread are computed at the 2-hourly steps.
- **Diagnostics, not headline:**
  - Linear interpolation, as a sensitivity row.
  - The daily-mean score against the daily-mean truth, which separates the
    estimation error from the interpolation error.
  - Known bias: a daily mean is not the mid-day instantaneous value. The
    difference is of order curvature × (1 day)² / 24, small at the forced
    scales and largest for fast eddies. A mean-preserving (histopolating)
    spline is the fix if the diagnostic shows it matters.
- **Consequence:** the DA rows keep their current 2-hourly scores. **No DA
  re-run is needed** (the v1 piece P2 is dropped).

### 2.3 S1 without observation error: DA re-run

- **New variant.** `base_noobs` in `REALISTIC_VARIANTS`
  (`evaluation/qg_specwind_s1.py`) = the realistic base with
  `obs_white_frac = obs_corr_frac = 0`. That leaves:
  - wind amplitude +15%, random wind error 25%, position error 50 km;
  - rd −10%, drag −50%;
  - DA grid 32 × 32.
- **Same draws.** `corrupt_obs` is then a no-op, so the observations are the S0
  ones and the filter's R is the S0 R (5%). The common-random-number seeding
  gives each component its own generator, so the wind and parameter errors are
  draw-for-draw the ones in the current S1 rows.
- **Runs.** ETKF, EnKF, EnKS and 4D-Var (strong, and weak at the S1-tuned
  point) on 100 test windows, forced and coupled, through
  `batch/run_qg_specwind_da.sbatch` with `PRESET=realistic KAPPA=1
  VARIANT=base_noobs`. Outputs go to their own `_rbase_noobs` directories.
- **Re-tuning on val.** R × 6 was chosen with the obs error present and partly
  absorbs it. The R scale is re-selected on the 20 val windows over
  {1, 2, 4, 6, 8} for the ensembles, and the weak-4D-Var Q scale over
  {0.03, 0.1, 0.3}.
- **Report.** The current S1 rows stay as a labelled variant ("S1 + altimetry
  error"), and the new rows become the S1 columns.
- **Expected effect.**
  - In the Shapley budget the obs group is 31% of the ETKF S0 → S1 loss, and
    the interactions are about 0. The ETKF S1 score should therefore rise from
    0.579 to about 0.64.
  - This moves S1 away from the user's earlier calibration target (ETKF
    upper-layer ψ EV ≈ 0.85).
  - **Open point** (§9 #7): accept the milder S1, or scale up the model-error
    components (e.g. the `mid`/`high` variants without obs) to restore the
    target.
- **Cost.**
  - Ensembles: about 4 GPU-hours per method and test set at N = 80, so
    3 × 2 × 4 ≈ 24.
  - Plus the S1-tuned ensemble rows (≈ 24) and 4D-Var (≈ 10).
  - Plus the val re-tuning (≈ 6).
  - **About 60 GPU-hours**, as throttled arrays.

## 3. Schemes (round 1)

All rows use the monai T-channels backbone (`MonaiUNet2DQGSolver`, days merged
into channels, circular convolutions) at the **M tier**, hidden [64, 128, 256].
This follows the monai-default rule and matches the legacy Q1. Parameter counts
are measured and reported.

| id | family | inputs | output | notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **G1** | DirectUNet | obs | ψ (one pass) | `direct_unet_tchannels`, the legacy Q1 recipe |
| **G2** | Vanilla CFM | obs | ψ ensemble | a generative flow on the T-channels backbone, **`train_tau_0_only: false`**. Sampler `ens30_no20`: 30 members × 20 early-fine Euler steps, τ_k = 1 − (1 − k/N)^0.5, the L96 benchmark sampler. Plus a τ = 0 mean row |
| **G3** | SDA1 | none at training; obs through guidance | ψ ensemble | `UnconditionalPriorCFM` on the 2D backbone. Guided `ens30`, 10 steps; guidance weight and R scale tuned on val |

Round 2 candidates (§8): obs + IC (G1b), PredictStateCFM, conditioned
DirectUNet (G1c) and conditioned SDA (SDA3-fix).

### 3.1 DirectUNet capacity tiers (user, 2026-10-04)

G1 is also trained at the S and L tiers. The ladder changes the width only, at
2 residual blocks per level, with `norm_num_groups=4` and dropout 0.1 as at M.
Counts and costs were measured on the G1 model with the obs mask, on an RTX 8000,
as forward + backward at batch 2:

| tier | id | hidden | res blocks | params | ms / step (B = 2) | peak memory |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| S | **G1-S** | [32, 64, 128] | 2 | 3.68 M | 72 | 0.22 GiB |
| M | **G1** (headline) | [64, 128, 256] | 2 | 14.54 M | 74 | 0.41 GiB |
| L | **G1-L** | [128, 256, 512] | 2 | 57.84 M | 77 | 0.96 GiB |

- S is the L96 "S+" tier (width [32, 64, 128] at 2 blocks), so the ladder is a
  pure ×4-per-step width ladder with M in the middle.
- At batch 2 on the 64 × 64 grid, the step time is set by kernel launches, not
  by width. L therefore costs about the same per epoch as M, and the
  epoch time is likely dominated by regeneration (E0 confirms).
- **Question answered:** whether the M-tier G1 is capacity-limited (L better
  outside the bootstrap interval) or over-sized (S within the interval). L96
  saw a collapse at L before the cosine schedule (2026-09-10), so L also checks
  that the schedule keeps it stable here.
- **Scope:** DirectUNet only. G2 and G3 stay at M in round 1. If G1-L beats G1
  clearly, an L-tier flow is a round-2 item (§8).
- **Protocol:** the same as G1 (§4.1), with G1-M's λ_q (λ_q is a loss-scale
  choice and is not re-swept per tier), 200 epochs, cosine schedule, fixed
  val draw for checkpoint selection. 1 seed first, then 3 seeds for the
  report rows (E5).

### 4.2 Setting the q-loss weight λ_q empirically

- **Do not reuse the legacy 2.54e9.** It is 1/Var(q) of the storm-forced split.
  Here the forcing, the parameter ranges and the calm windows all change the
  scale.
- **Step 1 — measure.** Var(q_daily) and Var(ψₙ) on the first regeneration
  draw. Set λ₀ = 1/Var(q_daily), which makes both terms O(1) at initialization.
- **Step 2 — sweep on G1.** λ_q / λ₀ ∈ {0, 0.1, 0.3, 1, 3, 10}, 50 epochs each,
  with a cosine schedule over those 50 epochs. Select by the **val benchmark
  score**: the mean of the four EVs at 2 hours, after interpolation.
  - The score weights q₁ and q₂ like ψ₁ and ψ₂, so the weight trades ψ skill
    against q skill directly.
  - Ties within the bootstrap interval go to the smaller λ.
  - Also logged: the two loss terms' ratio over training, and the per-field val
    EVs. The trade-off is reported, not just the winner.
- **Step 3 — reuse.** G2 and G3 use G1's λ_q, with a check at λ/3 and 3λ on G2
  only. A large shift there means the CFM endpoint weighting (below) matters and
  G3 gets its own check.
- **The q term in the flows.**
  - **Where it applies.** G2 and G3 train on random τ, so the q term is applied
    to the endpoint prediction x̂₁ = x_τ + (1 − τ) v.
  - **Why the flow target is unchanged.** q = L ψ is linear and fixed per
    window. The q-MSE on x̂₁ is then a reweighted ψ-MSE with the same
    minimizer, E[x₁ | x_τ, y]. Only the relative weight across spatial scales
    changes (L amplifies small scales).
  - **The τ-weighting.** The x̂₁ error is (1 − τ) × the velocity error, so the
    q term weighs early τ most, where the flow decides the large scales. If
    this destabilizes training, the fallback is to apply it only for τ ≥ 0.5.
  - **Precedent.** This differs from the legacy QG CFM, which was τ = 0 only.

### 4.3 Training observing system: the randomization

The noise model is fixed: 5% white, × the window's ψ₁ std, the S0/S1 test
model. No correlated error. **Only the sampling is randomized.** Each training
sample is a window from the current regeneration round plus **a fresh
observation draw at every `__getitem__`**: per window and per epoch, never
frozen.

| factor | distribution | covers | implementation |
|---|---|---|---|
| density: columns per day C | **uniform integer on {3, …, 30}** (user, 2026-10-04): 4.7% to 47% of the grid per day | the 3-column headline (the sparse end) up to dense, swath-like coverage | `--cols-per-day-min 3 --cols-per-day-max 30` |
| geometry | **C distinct (step, column) slots per day, drawn uniformly without replacement from all 12 × 64 = 768 slots of the day** (user, 2026-10-04). Several columns may share a step; a column may recur at another step of the same day; no slot is drawn twice | full uniform coverage of the day's space-time slots at every density | `--train-cols-sampling uniform_slots` (implemented, §5) |
| noise realization | a fresh 5% white draw per observation | the test noise model | `obs_noise_std_frac = 0.05` (exists) |

- **Why randomize the density:** frozen per-window obs cost DirectUNet-M about
  20% RMSE in L96, and dense draws give the network well-constrained windows to
  learn the ψ₁ → (ψ₂, q) mapping from. C is visible to the network through the
  observation mask.
- **The headline density sits at the sparse edge of the range.** The test (3
  columns per day) is the hardest case the network sees, and only 1 draw in 28.
  This is the main risk of this choice (R3); the fixed-C ablation measures it.
  If it costs skill at 3 columns per day, the fallback is to sample C
  log-uniformly (more weight near 3), not to narrow the range.
- **Train vs test geometry.** Test (and the DA rows) keep the `sequential`
  sampler: C distinct columns, each at its own step, so no two columns share a
  step. At C = 3, a uniform-slot draw differs from it only when two columns share
  a step or a column repeats. That happens in about 1 − (11/12 × 10/12) ×
  (63/64 × 62/64) ≈ 27% of days, so the headline test geometry stays
  well inside the training distribution. The networks only ever see daily bins:
  two columns at one step look the same as at two steps, and a repeated column
  is averaged in its bin. So the difference reduces to "C distinct columns" vs
  "≤ C distinct columns" per day.
- **Implementation (done):**
  - `cols_sampling = "uniform_slots"` in `data/qg.py`
    (`_generate_uniform_slot_column_observations`), with the same per-time
    column-list storage as the existing `random` mode.
  - `expand_obs_to_grid` now reads that list storage. Before, it only handled
    one column per step, so the `random` mode could not be used on the neural
    path at all.
  - `QGNeuralDataset(cols_sampling=...)` overrides the sampler for one dataset's
    on-the-fly draws only (the train split). The density check allows up to
    12 × 64 slots under the new mode.
  - `train_qg_neural.py --train-cols-sampling` (YAML: `data.train_cols_sampling`),
    persisted in `resolved_config.yaml`.
  - **Training split only:** val, test and the DA rows keep the `sequential`
    sampler; the DA code is unchanged.
  - Tests cover: distinct slots per day, C > 12, shared steps, the pooled
    uniformity over steps and columns, the grid expansion and the dataset
    override.
- **Validation (fixed, for checkpoint selection and the λ_q sweep):** one fixed
  draw per val window at C = 3, 5% white, the test setting.
- **Ablation, after the headline rows:** G1 trained at fixed C = 3. It measures
  what the 3–30 randomization costs or gains at the headline density.
- **Density curve (E6):** the DA curve covers 1–6 columns per day. The networks
  are evaluated at 3, 4, 6, 12 (`sequential`) and 30 (`uniform_slots`, since
  `sequential` caps at 12). 1 and 2 would be outside the training range and
  are reported only as extrapolation.
- **Fixed val draw (done):** `_build_specwind_data` now builds val with
  `with_fixed_obs` and `on_the_fly_obs=False`, as for test (it used to redraw
  the val obs on every pass).

### 4.4 Family-specific settings

| | G1 DirectUNet | G2 Vanilla CFM | G3 SDA1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| model | `MonaiDirectUNetQGChannelTime` | new: Vanilla CFM on `MonaiUNet2DQGSolver` with τ embedding (the backbone already accepts `tau`); obs + mask as input channels, like G1 | `UnconditionalPriorCFM` on `MonaiUNet2DQGSolver`, no obs channels |

| tuned on val | λ_q (§4.2) | λ_q check | guidance weight gw ∈ {5, 10, 25, 50, 100} × R scale ∈ {0.5, 1, 2} relative to the nominal obs-error variance (0.05² × ψ₁ std², the DA's R), on 20 val windows; 20 steps if 10 is unstable |

- **SDA obs operator:** the state is flattened to (30 days, 8192). The guidance
  is the daily-binned ψ₁ mask (`guided_obs_cost` with a per-day
  `obs_channel_mask`), on the same grid the DirectUNet ingests.
- **SDA and the randomization:** SDA never sees observations in training, so
  §4.3 does not apply to it. The observation model enters only through R at
  sampling time.
- **S1 = S0 for all three families** (§1, N2): none uses the DA model or the
  wind.

## 8. Round 2 (deferred by decision)

- **Initial-state input:** G1b (obs + lagged IC) and the CFM with IC. SDA would
  take the IC as a day-0 pseudo-observation in the guidance.
- **Conditioning on the wind and parameters:** G1c and SDA3-fix, trained with
  noisy-severity conditioning and read with true inputs (S0) or the DA model's
  corrupted inputs (S1). This needs a port of the spectral S1 corruption into G5,
  which today supports only `cond_mode` none/true.
- **PredictStateCFM**, the best L96 flow.
- **L-tier flows** (G2-L, and G3-L if relevant), if G1-L beats G1-M outside the bootstrap interval (§3.1).
- a longer training budget if E2–E4 are still
  improving at 200 epochs.

## 9. Decisions

| # | decision (2026-10-04) |
|---|---|
| 1 | Train on **daily means** (ψ and q loss terms). Score on the **2-hourly truth** after **cubic interpolation** of the network outputs in time; no DA re-run |
| 2 | **S0 and S1 share the S0 obs noise** (5% white). The training randomization covers density (**3–30 columns per day**) and geometry only (§4.3) |
| 3 | **No initial-state input** in round 1 |
| 4 | **Vanilla CFM first**; PredictState deferred |
| 5 | **No conditioning** in round 1 |
| 6 | Cosine annealing; loss = ψ-MSE + λ_q q-MSE, with **λ_q set empirically** (§4.2); 200 epochs to start |
| 7 | **Re-run the DA S1 rows without obs error** (`base_noobs`, §2.3); current rows kept as a labelled variant. *Open:* accept the milder S1, or scale up the model error to restore the ETKF ψ₁ ≈ 0.85 target |
| 8 | **DirectUNet capacity sweep:** G1 also at the S ([32, 64, 128]) and L ([128, 256, 512]) tiers, 2 res blocks, same protocol and λ_q as M (§3.1). G2 / G3 stay at M in round 1 |

## 10. Risks

| # | risk | mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | Interpolating daily means loses intra-day signal, so the network rows are capped below their daily-mean skill. | The daily-mean diagnostic gives the gap; mean-preserving spline if it matters (§2.2). |
| R2 | ψ energy varies across windows (legacy storm QG: 24× / 83× for layers 1 / 2; larger here with the calm windows). Global z-scoring under-weights calm windows. | Calm / windy strata (report §7); per-window scale only as an ablation. |
| R3 | The test density (3) is the sparse edge of the 3–30 training range, so the network may under-fit it. | The fixed-C ablation (E6) measures it; fall back to a log-uniform C. |
| R8 | Without obs error, S1 is milder than its calibration target. | Decision 7: keep `base_noobs`, or move to a stronger model-error variant without obs. |
| R4 | SDA guidance on 8192 × 30 dims is unstable or too weak at 10 steps. | Val sweep (E4); 20 steps; cap the guidance step norm. |
| R5 | Regeneration dominates the epoch time. | E0 measures it; raise `--regen-every`. |
| R6 | Val re-materialization lands on a non-rtx8000 node. | `--constraint` in the sbatch; the loader raises on mismatch. |
| R7 | The stored test split is read for tuning by mistake. | Tuning only on val; `materialize_split(test)` only in P1. |
