## 2026-09-26: G5 — train-time regeneration of QG spectral-wind windows

**Summary:** Implements decision 5 of
`docs/plans/tech/qg_batched_generation_datasets.md`: the QG neural pipeline
can train on the spectral-wind dataset with fresh train windows drawn every
k epochs. New module `data/qg_specwind_neural.py`:
- `legacy_windows`: full-resolution G2 windows to the legacy window-dict
  layout that `QGNeuralDataset` and `_generate_obs_ic` consume
  (`init_lead_truth` of 121 frames, `true_state` of 360, `true_params`,
  optional `wind_curl` from the 12 amplitudes);
- `SpecWindTrainSource`: per round, fresh spawn keys at index ≥ 10⁹ in the
  train namespace and an independent Latin hypercube;
- `materialize_split`: val by deterministic regeneration at full resolution,
  checked against the stored frames; test read from storage, purpose
  `test` only;
- `train_norm_stats`: train-only psi, forcing and parameter stats;
- `RegenerateTrainWindows`: a Lightning callback that swaps the windows.

`train_qg_neural.py` gains `--specwind-spec`, `--specwind-root`,
`--regen-windows`, `--regen-every` and `--regen-batch-size`. The legacy
path is unchanged when they are absent. `data/qg_datasets.py`:
`generate_windows` accepts custom factors and a `keep_every` override, and
`design_factors` accepts `n` and `design_key` (defaults unchanged).

**Files modified:** `data/qg_specwind_neural.py` and
`tests/test_qg_specwind_neural.py` (8 tests) — new; `train_qg_neural.py`;
`data/qg_datasets.py`; `docs/plans/tech/qg_batched_generation_datasets.md`
(§4.6 note).
**Rationale:** Train diversity is capped by disk (the stored v1 train split
is 13 GB). Regeneration draws new windows from the same factor box every
few epochs, and val and test stay frozen. Findings:
- **Val re-materialized on GPU is bit-identical to its stored 12-hourly
  frames** (max abs difference 0.0), so the stored manifests plus the
  generator are the source of truth.
- **A regeneration round costs about 30 s** (gyrostat, launch-bound) plus
  0.26 s per window: about 6.5 min per 1000 windows on an RTX 8000.

`noisy` and `scenario` conditioning are refused under `--specwind-spec`:
they need the spectral S1 corruption (Option B PR-2).
