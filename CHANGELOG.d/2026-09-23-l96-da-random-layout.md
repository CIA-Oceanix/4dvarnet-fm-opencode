## 2026-09-23: L96 DA baselines under the random observing system (per-channel missing obs)

**Summary:** ETKF/EnKF/Strong-4DVar can now assimilate obs rows with missing channels, and a new sweep scores them under the training random observing system (`data.obs_random_layout`: stratified obs times, k observed fast channels per window, subset redrawn per obs time) with step 0 always observed, at least one obs time in every 500-step DA window, and the step-0 missing fast channels filled by linear interpolation along the fast ring.
**Files modified:** `evaluation/baselines.py` — batched ETKF drops a window's NaN channels from HA/dy, batched EnKF masks them exactly (block-diagonal innovation covariance, zero gain and innovation), Weak/Strong-4DVar obs cost masks per channel (`_channel_obs_mask`), new `init_fill` hook on Weak4DVar/Strong4DVar/ETKF/EnKF (`_obs0_for_init` raises on a NaN step-0 vector without it); `evaluation/run_l96.py` — `make_fast_ring_fill`; `data/obs_times.py` — unchanged: step 0 is pinned with master's `first_step` (PR #242), which reproduces the arrays' layouts bit for bit; `eval_da_random_layout_l96.py` — new, one layout cell per call (`--n-obs-range`, `--fast-range`, `--inflation`, `--cases`), DA-window coverage by whole-draw rejection, saves the drawn layouts for scoring learned schemes on the same obs; `batch/run_l96_da_random_layout.sbatch` (array 54845: arms A U{6..50} / A' U{10..100}, n_obs {10..100} x k {4..16} grid, inflation check) + `batch/run_l96_da_random_layout_s0_inf15.sbatch` (array 54954: S0 ETKF/EnKF at inflation 1.5); `reports/l96/generate_l96_da_random_layout_report.py` + `reports/l96/outputs/l96_da_random_layout{.md,.png,_s0_inf15.png}`; `tests/test_da_random_layout_l96.py` — 11 tests.
**Rationale:** Before this, a NaN channel inside an observed row poisoned the ETKF update, collapsed the EnKF ensemble to zeros (`nan_to_num`) and made 4D-Var fit the missing channels to 0 -- silent on the regular grid, where rows are complete. Masking is an exact no-op on complete rows (DA golden values unchanged) and equals running with an operator that does not observe the missing channels (tested for ETKF/EnKF). n_obs=5 was dropped: it can leave a DA window without obs, where Strong-4DVar only free-runs (the coverage rejection only binds for n_obs 7-9). Results: on S1 the fixed P1 inflation 2.0 is the right choice, on S0 1.5 is best at both n_obs 10 and 100 (2.0 over-disperses and makes EnKF diverge at high n_obs / low k: 3.06 at n_obs 100, k 4). Arm A S0 (inflation 1.5 for the filters): ETKF 0.881, Strong-4DVar 0.906, EnKF 0.916; arm A' S0: Strong-4DVar 0.743, ETKF 0.796, EnKF 0.850; S1 at dense obs the filters beat Strong-4DVar (ETKF 1.05 vs 1.39 at n_obs 100, k 16), which saturates at its model-error floor.
