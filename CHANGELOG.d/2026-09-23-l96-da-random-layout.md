## 2026-09-23: L96 DA baselines under the random observing system (per-channel missing obs)

**Summary:** ETKF/EnKF/Strong-4DVar can now assimilate obs rows with missing channels, and a new sweep scores them under the training random observing system (`data.obs_random_layout`: n_obs U{5..50} at stratified times, k U{4..16} fast channels per window) with step 0 always observed and the step-0 missing fast channels filled by interpolation along the fast ring.
**Files modified:** `evaluation/baselines.py` — batched ETKF drops a window's NaN channels from HA/dy, batched EnKF masks them exactly (block-diagonal innovation covariance, zero gain and innovation), Weak/Strong-4DVar obs cost masks per channel (`_channel_obs_mask`), new `init_fill` hook on Weak4DVar/Strong4DVar/ETKF/EnKF (`_obs0_for_init` raises on a NaN step-0 vector without it); `evaluation/run_l96.py` — `make_fast_ring_fill`; `data/obs_times.py` — `pin_first` on `stratified_obs_times`/`resample_obs_variable` (default off, training unchanged); `eval_da_random_layout_l96.py` — new, one layout cell per call, saves the drawn layouts for scoring learned schemes on the same obs; `batch/run_l96_da_random_layout.sbatch` — arm A (200 windows, random layout), arm B (n_obs x k grid), inflation check; `tests/test_da_random_layout_l96.py` — 9 tests.
**Rationale:** Before this, a NaN channel inside an observed row poisoned the ETKF update, collapsed the EnKF ensemble to zeros (`nan_to_num`) and made 4D-Var fit the missing channels to 0 -- silent on the regular grid, where rows are complete. Masking is an exact no-op on complete rows (DA golden values unchanged) and equals running with an operator that does not observe the missing channels (tested for ETKF/EnKF). The spatial ring fill uses the noisy step-0 obs of the observed fast channels, with no extra initial noise beyond P1's per-member obs-noise perturbation; the 16 never-observed fast channels keep the P1 initialization.
