## 2026-09-23: τ-consistency probes of the CFM operator + next-steps draft

**Summary:** Two read-only probes explain why the P1 flows' ODE ensemble mean beats their own τ=0 prediction of `E[x1|y]`, and measure which τ-consistency identities of the exact operator `D = E[x1|x_tau,y]` the learned one breaks. Results note and a draft plan for training interventions.
**Files modified:** `reports/l96/probe_ode_mean_along_path.py` — per-step posterior-mean predictions along the Euler path; `reports/l96/probe_tau_consistency.py` — E1 orthogonality, B2 path moments, B4 total-variance conservation, A1/A3 Jacobian limits, C1 Jacobian symmetry; `reports/l96/outputs/cfm_tau_consistency/*.json` — S0 outputs for PredictStateCFM-M and VanillaCFM-M; `docs/results/cfm_tau_consistency.md` — results; `docs/scoping/cfm_tau_consistency_next_steps.md` — draft plan; `docs/scoping/README.md` — index row.
**Rationale:** The gain is not averaging over τ: per-step errors correlate at 0.90–0.99, and the Euler endpoint is exactly the last prediction. The τ=0 prediction is biased (E1 slope 0.94 / 1.12, should be 0) and is corrected within 1–2 steps (implicit self-conditioning). Separately, total variance decays 2.6–3.2× over τ ∈ [0.1, 0.4] while the transport stays self-consistent. That locates the flows' under-dispersion in the low/mid-τ operator.
**Verification:** Both probes ran end to end on the P1 M-tier checkpoints (S0, 200 windows, 30 members). The finite-difference JVP (eps=1e-2) matches exact autograd to <0.1%. `ruff check` is clean on both scripts, and `pytest tests/ -m "not slow"` passes (see PR).
