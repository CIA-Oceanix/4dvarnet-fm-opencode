## 2026-09-27: DA-3/DA-4 test benchmark (S0 + realistic S1) and S1 design, calibration and attribution for the spectral-wind QG DA

**Summary:** Runs the S0 benchmark on test, and adds S1 with four
independent error components, calibrated on val and attributed with
Shapley values. ETKF/EnKF only; 4D-Var deferred.
- **`evaluation/qg_specwind_s1.py`** (new): S1 error components, the
  spectral analogues of the legacy QG S1:
  - `amp`: wind amplitude bias;
  - `noise`: per-mode OU wind noise scaled by the train-split mode RMS;
  - `shift`: OU translation of the wind pattern;
  - `param`: rd and bottom-drag bias.

  Each component has its own generator per window (common random numbers).
  The wind is corrupted continuously over the lead buffer and the window,
  so the bred ensemble also sees the wrong wind (`wind_lead_da`).
- **`evaluation/run_qg_specwind_da.py`:**
  - `window_metrics` and `evaluate` moved here, so every run records
    per-window EVs with each window's own parameters (the DA-2 sweep reuses
    them);
  - `--s1-kappa`, `--s1-components` and per-component level overrides.
- **`evaluation/qg_specwind_s1_sweep.py`** + `batch/run_qg_specwind_s1.sbatch`
  (new): val calibration (single components, and κ scans of the full mix)
  and a 16-subset Shapley attribution.
- **`reports/qg/generate_qg_specwind_da_report.py`** (new): DA-3/DA-4 tables.
  They cover main and bridge densities, D2 forced vs coupled, D4 density,
  D3 strata, and S1 against S0.
- **`batch/run_qg_specwind_da.sbatch`:** `DISP`, `KAPPA`, `PRESET` and
  `VARIANT`.
- **Realism-anchored S1** (`REALISTIC_VARIANTS`): separate `rd` and `drag`
  biases; an `obs` component (extra white noise plus per-pass correlated
  offset and tilt, with the filter's R at the total variance); and a `res`
  component, a coarser DA grid for which the bred ensemble now breeds on the
  DA grid (`_bred_ensemble(lead_truth=...)`). Groups for the attribution:
  forcing / rd / drag / obs / res.
- **Calibration and attribution on val:**
  `docs/results/qg_specwind_da_s1_calibration.md`.
  - **Legacy-analogue κ = 2** meets the target medians (ψ₁ 0.83, q₁ 0.14),
    but 69% of its loss is the parameter bias, driven by an rd error about
    3× real uncertainty.
  - **Realistic base** is too mild (q₁ 0.37). Only its upper edge ("high")
    reaches the target (ψ₁ 0.85, 65% of windows in range; q₁ 0.17).
  - **Attribution at "high":** observation error 44% of the score loss
    (55% of q₁), rd 23% (compounding: its Shapley value is 2× its
    stand-alone loss), resolution 16%, forcing 10% (but 32% of ψ₂), drag 8%.
  - **Interpolated variants:** `mid25` / `mid50` / `mid75` lie between base
    and high (`interpolate`). Across them ψ₁ moves little (0.865 → 0.817)
    and q₁ about 3× as much (0.338 → 0.163).
  - **Selected S1: realistic base** (`REALISTIC_SELECTED`), at the user's
    revised aim of ETKF ψ₁ ≈ 0.85–0.9. Attribution at base: obs 43%,
    resolution 22%, rd 16%, drag 10%, forcing 9% of the score loss. The
    κ = 2 test runs were dropped.
- **DA-3/DA-4 on test** (100 windows per cell, forced and coupled):
  `reports/qg/outputs/qg_specwind_da_report.md`,
  `docs/results/qg_specwind_da_s0_s1_test.md`.
  - **S0 score:** ETKF 0.766, EnKF 0.761, free forecast 0.398. No
    forced–coupled difference (D2).
  - **Density curve** (score by columns per day, D4): 1 → 0.663,
    2 → 0.727, 3 → 0.766, 4 → 0.792, 6 → 0.820.
  - **Realistic S1 (base):** ETKF ψ₁ 0.854, q₁ 0.390. The EnKF is more
    robust (score 0.596 against 0.559), and calm windows are hurt most in
    ψ₂.
- **Inputs hard-linked** into the main checkout's `experiments/` (not
  bound to this worktree).
- **Report restructured** in the layout of the legacy `qg_da_report.md`, and
  added to the Headline reports as the QG-gyrostat reference. It has:
  - per-scenario DA tables, ranked among the DA methods with the free
    forecast as a reference row;
  - a configuration synthesis and the S1 error budget;
  - density, forced vs coupled, and strata sections;
  - best/median/worst reconstructions with DA-cycle GIFs.
- **New `reports/qg/generate_qg_specwind_da_figs.py`:**
  - it re-runs ETKF and EnKF on the 3 example windows in S0 and S1 for the
    reconstruction panels and GIFs (under S1 the GIF shows the true and the
    DA-model wind curl);
  - summary figures: density curve, S0 vs S1, S1 budget.
- **`evaluation/run_qg_baselines._bred_ensemble`:** uses `wind_lead_da`
  when given.
- **Initial-spread check:** the initial spread is 0.39× the actual
  initial error, so `disp_frac` 2 and 2.5 were tested on val
  (`evaluation/qg_specwind_da2_sweep.py` tasks 92–95). There was no gain
  (ETKF Δ −0.004 [−0.013, +0.005]) and 2.5 is harmful, so `disp_frac`
  stays 1.
