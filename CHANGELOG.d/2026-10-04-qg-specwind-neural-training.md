## 2026-10-04: QG gyrostat neural round 1 — training side (G1 S/M/L, G2 CFM, G3 SDA prior)

**Summary:** Training code and configs for the learned rows of the spectral-wind gyrostat QG benchmark (design: `docs/plans/analysis/qg_specwind_neural.md`, added here): a `uniform_slots` train-time column sampler (3–30 columns per day, fresh draw per sample), an obs-mask input, the empirical q-loss weight λ_q = scale / Var(q_daily), a fixed val obs draw, the random-τ Vanilla CFM (G2) and the unconditional SDA prior (G3) on the T-channels backbone, and S / M / L capacity tiers for the DirectUNet (G1).
**Files modified:** `data/qg.py` — `uniform_slots` sampler, multi-column-per-step `expand_obs_to_grid`; `data/qg_neural.py` — train-split-only `cols_sampling` override; `models/monai_unet_qg2d.py` — `use_obs_mask`, `MonaiVanillaCFMQGChannelTime`, `MonaiSDAPriorQGChannelTime`; `train_qg_neural.py` — flow training branch (q term on x̂₁), `--q-loss-weight-scale`, `--train-cols-sampling`, YAML `data.cols_per_day`, `model.hidden_channels` / `model.num_res_blocks` now honoured for the T-channels types (refused for the others), fixed val draw; `evaluation/qg_runs.py`, `eval_qg_neural_s0_s1.py` — read the tier and obs-mask fields back; `config/experiment/G{1,1S,1L,2,3}_*_specwind.yaml`; `batch/run_qg_specwind_neural_train.sbatch`; tests in `tests/test_qg_neural.py`, `tests/test_qg_cols_sampling.py`, `tests/test_qg_config_persistence.py`.
**Rationale:** Round 1 of the plan (decisions 1–8): obs-only networks next to the gyrostat DA rows. `model.hidden_channels` used to be a dead YAML field (every model built at [64, 128, 256]); it now selects the tier for the T-channels types, and the built value is recorded in `resolved_config.yaml` and read back at evaluation. Measured tiers (G1 with mask): S [32, 64, 128] 3.68 M, M 14.54 M, L [128, 256, 512] 57.84 M params; 72 / 74 / 77 ms per training step at batch 2 on an RTX 8000. The evaluation driver (P1), the S1-without-obs-error DA re-runs (P2) and the SDA guidance sampler (P6) follow in separate PRs.
**Verification:** `pytest tests/ -m "not slow"`; `ruff check` on the touched files; E0 smoke runs (2 epochs, G1 / G2 / G3) on the gyrostat dataset.
