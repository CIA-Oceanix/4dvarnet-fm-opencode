## 2026-10-12: L96 DirectUNet with a 2D (time x sector) circular backbone

**Summary:** New model type `monai_direct_unet_2d` (`models/monai_unet_l96_2d.py::MonaiDirectUNet2D`): the 24-D observed L96 state is laid out on a (T, NO=8) grid with channels [X_k, Y_k0, Y_k1] per sector and processed by MONAI's `DiffusionModelUNet` (spatial_dims=2) with circular padding along sectors, zero padding along time, time-only down/upsampling and per-sector time-axial bottleneck attention. The network is exactly equivariant to cyclic sector shifts. Configs `L96B_directunet2d_monaiM` (parameter-matched to DirectUNet-M: [40, 80, 160], GroupNorm 8, 5.63 M vs 5.89 M) `L96B_directunet2d_monaiMwide` (DirectUNet-M widths, 14.4 M) and the capacity tiers `L96B_directunet2d_monaiS` ([24, 48, 96], 2.03 M) and `L96B_directunet2d_monaiL` ([80, 160, 320], GroupNorm 16, 22.5 M, vs 23.5 M for 1D DU-L), all on the benchmark default; `batch/run_l96b_du2d_seeds.sbatch` trains seeds 1-3 and runs the DirectUNet-M deterministic eval on both test sets.
**Files modified:** `models/monai_unet_l96_2d.py` — new backbone + model; `train.py`, `training/lightning_module.py` — dispatch for `monai_direct_unet_2d`; `config/experiment/L96B_directunet2d_monai{S,M,Mwide,L}.yaml`; `batch/run_l96b_du2d_seeds.sbatch`; `tests/test_monai_unet_l96_2d.py`.
**Rationale:** Every L96 backbone convolves over time only with all 24 variables as channels, so each sector's dynamics is learned separately. The L96 problem (dynamics, scalar forcing, `fast_weights`, `obs_j` pattern) is invariant to cyclic sector shifts; the original 4DVarNet L96 experiments used 2D convolutions. First step of testing a 2D circular backbone on DirectUNet before FDV1/FDV2 (where the prior Phi is the component most likely to benefit). Design choices: the fast index j stays a channel (`fast_weights` differ per j); the sector axis is never downsampled (stride 2 would break equivariance to odd shifts); circular padding is set per module instead of through MONAI's global conv factory, which `models/monai_unet_qg2d.py` already overrides with both-axis circular padding (time must not wrap). Full 2D bottleneck attention (6000 tokens) needs 43 GB without flash attention and makes a training step 2.0x slower than time-axial attention (2.2 s vs 1.09 s on an RTX 8000, batch 16), so `mid_attention: time` is the default, `full` and `none` are options. Per-channel normalisation stats are equal across sectors to two decimals, so equivariance holds in normalised units to that precision.
**Verification:** `pytest tests/test_monai_unet_l96_2d.py tests/test_monai_unet_adapter.py tests/test_monai_unet_qg2d.py` (equivariance error 3e-8 for 1- and 3-sector shifts in all three attention modes; sector axis never downsampled; time not periodic at the conv level; configs build with the expected parameter counts); 2-epoch smoke run of `train.py` + `eval_neural_l96.py` on the RTX 8000 (72 s/epoch, val loss 0.51 -> 0.38). Training job 60137 (seeds 1-3, A100) submitted; results to follow.
