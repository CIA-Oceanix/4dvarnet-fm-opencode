## 2026-09-30: S1 tuning of the QG gyrostat DA — inflation diverges, R × 6 absorbs the model error

**Summary:** The ETKF, EnKF and localized EnKS were tuned on val under the
realistic S1 (44 + 12 configurations):
- **Multiplicative inflation diverges.** It is applied at every one of the
  about 90 analyses per window: 1.05 already costs 0.23, and 1.1 and above
  blow up.
- **Scaling the filter's observation-error variance helps every method,**
  with a plateau at 6–8. Selected: R × 6.

Test (100 windows, S1), S0-tuned → S1-tuned, forced / coupled:
- ETKF 0.579 / 0.586 → 0.633 / 0.642;
- EnKF 0.596 / 0.604 → 0.634 / 0.642 (a tie with the ETKF);
- EnKS 0.602 / 0.610 → 0.664 / 0.672.

Spread/RMSE in q₁ goes from about 0.65 to about 0.86. The report shows the
S1-tuned rows next to the S0-tuned ones.
