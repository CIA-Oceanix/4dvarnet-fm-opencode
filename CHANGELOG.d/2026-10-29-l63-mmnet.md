## 2026-10-29: L63 MM-Net (MSE data term, fixed curvature step)

**Summary:** Added MM-Net (Tran et al., Sparse MM-Net, lambda_2 = 0) for L63: an unrolled MM solver with a masked-MSE data term, a constant step gamma*p (no curvature network, no projection) and a learned conv prior Phi; trained with MSE(x, x_hat) + MSE(x, Phi(x)). Wired as `model_type: mmnet` and as an `MM-Net` row in `eval_neural_l63.py` (opt-in via `--methods MM-Net`).
**Files modified:** `models/mmnet.py` — new model; `train.py`, `training/lightning_module.py` — `mmnet` dispatch; `config/experiment/L63B_mmnet.yaml`, `L63B20_mmnet.yaml` — obs interval 10 / 20, same protocol as the D-Unet rows; `eval_neural_l63.py` — MM-Net row, checkpoints loaded only for the requested methods; `tests/test_mmnet.py` — new.
**Rationale:** MM-Net baseline for the L63 table. With p fixed the MM bound holds for the data term only (Hessian M <= I); `step`, `prior_weight` and `n_iter` are hyperparameters to tune on validation windows. The 1200-epoch runs are not trained yet.
**Verification:** `pytest tests/test_mmnet.py` — 5 passed; 2-epoch `train.py` smoke run and `eval_neural_l63.py --methods MM-Net` ran end-to-end (untrained weights). `ruff` is not installed in this env, so lint was not run.
