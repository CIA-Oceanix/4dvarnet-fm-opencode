## 2026-09-22: L96 training with stratified random observation times

**Summary:** New opt-in `data.obs_times_random` flag: each training window's regular 30-time obs grid is replaced, every batch, with the same number of obs times drawn one per 100-step block (stratified random), with fresh N(0, R_var) noise and all 24 channels observed at each time. Val/test keep the regular grid. Two P1-recipe arms added (DirectUNet-M, VanillaCFM-M) and launched as SLURM jobs 54677/54678.
**Files modified:** `data/obs_times.py` — new, `stratified_obs_times` + `resample_obs_times`; `data/dataloader.py` — `make_collate_fm(obs_times_cfg=...)`, applied before density augmentation and normalization; `train.py`, `conf/schema.py` — `data.obs_times_random` wiring (train loader only); `config/experiment/P1_directunet_monaiM_obstimes_l96.yaml`, `config/experiment/A1_vanillacfm_monaiM_obstimes_l96.yaml` — P1 configs + this flag only; `batch/run_l96_{p1_du_m,a1_vc_m}_obstimes_train.sbatch`; `tests/test_l96_obs_times.py` — 10 tests.
**Rationale:** Tests whether the obs-consuming learned schemes (which read obs only via `nan_to_num`, on the full 3000-step grid) overfit to the fixed 100-step spacing, and whether timing diversity regularizes them when scored on the unchanged regular-grid test set. SDA is unaffected (its prior never sees obs in training), so it is not retrained. Requires `states` in the observed subspace (raises with `full_state_target`).
**Verification:** `pytest tests/test_l96_obs_times.py tests/test_l96_normalization.py` — 26 passed; `ruff check` on touched files clean; 2-epoch smoke runs of both configs trained and evaluated end to end.
