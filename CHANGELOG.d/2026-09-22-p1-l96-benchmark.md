## 2026-09-22: P1 L96 benchmark — deterministic vs flow-matching vs SDA

**Summary:** Adds the P1 L96 benchmark: 16 trained rows across DirectUNet,
VanillaCFM, PredictStateCFM and SDA1/2/3 at the S+/M/L capacity tiers, all under
one declared recipe, with a dedicated report generator. Also fixes two bugs found
while producing it.

**Files modified:**
- `reports/l96/generate_p1_l96_benchmark.py` — new; emits
  `outputs/p1_l96_benchmark.md` + `.json` + `outputs/figures/p1_l96_hovm_*.png`.
  Metrics are recomputed from the stored ensembles rather than read from cached
  JSON, so the tables cannot drift from the artifacts. Covers the DA baselines
  (ETKF/EnKF/Strong-4DVar) alongside the learned schemes, reports BOTH S0 and S1
  with the S1/S0 robustness ratio, and renders Hovmöller reconstruction figures
  for the best/median/worst windows by reusing `plot_hovmoller`/`select_windows`
  from `generate_l96_consolidated_report.py` (imported, not duplicated). Each
  observation is rendered as an 11-column band: at 30 observations over a
  3000-step window a single column is ~0.15 px, so most vanished under
  rasterization and the survivors differed between the slow and fast panels,
  which read as two different observing systems. There is only one -- `obs_mask`
  is a single 1-D mask over time shared by every dimension; the slow/fast
  difference is SPATIAL (8 of 8 slow X, 16 of 32 fast Y). The widening is
  display-only and inflates the Obs row's "obs noise" label by ~1.7%
  (measured: half-width 5 costs +1.7%, half-width 10 costs +6.2%); the caption
  carries the undistorted values.
- `reports/l96/eval_mean_component.py` — new; scores each flow's tau=0 implied
  posterior mean as a deterministic point estimator.
- `evaluation/neural_inference.py` — new `eval_norm_stats(cfg)` helper.
- `train.py` — `evaluate_model` and the trajectory-saving path now dispatch
  `monai_predict_state_cfm` (2 sites).
- `config/experiment/{A1,A2,A3,B4,P1}_*.yaml` + matching `batch/*.sbatch` — new.

**Rationale:**
1. *The benchmark.* Every prior cross-family comparison mixed epoch budgets
   (DirectUNet 200 vs CFM/SDA 400), backbones and normalization. These rows fix
   all of it: monai backbone, `data.normalize: true`, cosine annealing, 400
   epochs, `batch_size: 16`, no obs-density augmentation. Unrolled/4DVarNet
   schemes are deliberately out of P1 scope.
2. *`train.py` dispatch.* `monai_predict_state_cfm` was the only monai
   model_type missing from `evaluate_model`'s elif chain, so the model trained
   for 400 epochs and then died with `UnboundLocalError: pred`. That is why no
   monai PredictStateCFM had ever been trained — it is now one of the
   best-performing families here.
3. *`eval_norm_stats`.* Evaluation paths that call `prepare_dataset` without
   `norm_stats` feed a `data.normalize: true` model RAW observations it never saw
   in training, then score its normalized-space output against raw truth. The
   symptom is an RMSE near 1.5-1.9 instead of ~0.35. `make_collate_eval`
   normalizes `obs` ONLY — `true_state` stays raw — so it is the MODEL side that
   must be converted, never the truth. The helper makes that the one obvious way
   to do it; `reports/l96/eval_mean_component.py` uses it.

**Verification:** `ruff check` clean on all touched files; `pytest -m "not slow"`
green. Unnormalized runs are bit-identical — the new branch is a no-op when
`norm_stats is None`.

**Headline** (S0, `all_obs`, 200 windows, physical units, per-window
convention): flow matching **0.3445** (VanillaCFM-M) vs deterministic **0.4699**
(DirectUNet-M) vs SDA **0.5063** (SDA1-M at the tuned `gw=20`) vs the best DA
baseline **0.7383** (Strong-4DVar) — flow matching 27% ahead of the deterministic
baseline at matched tier, parameters, schedule and data.

**The S1 column is the sharper result.** Every learned scheme is flat under model
error (S1/S0 ~ 1.00) because none of them uses a forward model; the DA baselines
degrade 1.69-1.95x since their operator carries the bias. That is a structural
difference, not a tuning one, and it is invisible in an S0-only table.

**Note on conventions:** this report uses the per-window `mean_d sqrt(mse_d)`
convention throughout, so its DA cells (ETKF S0 0.8662) differ from
`l96_consolidated_benchmark.md`'s pooled `evaluate_estimates` cells (ETKF S0
0.8883) for the same artifacts. Both are correct; they are different statistics
and must not be mixed across tables.
