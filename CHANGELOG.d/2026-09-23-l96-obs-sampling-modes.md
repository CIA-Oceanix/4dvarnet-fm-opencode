## 2026-09-23: L96 training obs sampling -- noise-redraw control and random observing system

**Summary:** Two new opt-in training-obs modes next to `data.obs_times_random` (#238), all train-only and redrawn every batch (val/test keep the regular 30-time, full-density grid): `data.obs_noise_redraw` keeps each window's regular obs times and redraws only the noise; `data.obs_random_layout` draws a random observing system per window -- obs count uniform in `obs_n_range` (default 5-50) at stratified times, and a fast-channel count uniform in `obs_fast_range` (default 4-16) drawn once per window (subset redrawn per obs time; slow channels always observed). The three modes are mutually exclusive. Code only; experiment configs and results follow separately.
**Files modified:** `data/obs_times.py` — `redraw_obs_noise`, `resample_obs_variable`; `data/dataloader.py` — `obs_times_cfg["mode"]` in {`stratified`, `noise_only`, `variable`}; `train.py`, `conf/schema.py` — flags and exclusivity check; `tests/test_l96_obs_times.py` — 5 new tests.
**Rationale:** P1 training obs (noise included) are generated once per window and reused every epoch. `noise_only` isolates the fresh-noise effect from the random-timing effect of `obs_times_random`; `variable` trains on sparser, partial observing systems.
**Verification:** `pytest tests/test_l96_obs_times.py` — 15 passed; `ruff check` on touched files clean; 2-epoch smoke train+eval with each mode.
