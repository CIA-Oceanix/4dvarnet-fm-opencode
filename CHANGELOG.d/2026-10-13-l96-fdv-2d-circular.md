## 2026-10-13: L96 FDV1/FDV2 and VanillaCFM with the 2D (time x sector) circular backbone

**Summary:** New `unet_backbone: monai_sector2d` for `FourDVarNetSolver` (`models/monai_unet_l96_2d.py::MonaiUNet2DSectorSolver`): each 24-channel block of the update input (and Phi's input) is laid out on the (T, NO) sector grid used by `MonaiDirectUNet2D`, so both the update network and the prior Phi are circular in sector and the whole unrolled solver is equivariant to cyclic sector shifts. Configs `L96B_fdv1_monai2dM` and `L96B_fdvsubgradmask_monai2dM_aux01det_frozen_philr01` (the parameter-matched 2D M widths [40, 80, 160], GroupNorm 8, on top of the 1D FDV1-M and stable FDV2-M recipes). New model type `monai_vanilla_cfm_2d` (`MonaiVanillaCFM2D`: VanillaCFM loss/sampler, velocity network on the sector grid with [x_tau, obs] as two blocks per sector), config `L96B_vanillacfm_monai2dM_ep1200` (5.64 M vs 5.89 M for VanillaCFM-M) and `batch/run_l96b_vcfm2d.sbatch` (train + the ens30 x 20-step flow eval of `L96B_vanillacfm_monaiM_ep1200`).
**Files modified:** `models/monai_unet_l96_2d.py` — FDV wrapper, `MonaiVanillaCFM2D`; `training/lightning_module.py` — dispatch; `batch/run_l96b_vcfm2d.sbatch`; `config/experiment/L96B_vanillacfm_monai2dM_ep1200.yaml`; `models/fourdvarnet.py` — `monai_sector2d` dispatch, `l96_NO`/`monai_mid_attention` args; `train.py`, `conf/schema.py` — pass-through; `config/experiment/L96B_fdv1_monai2dM.yaml`, `config/experiment/L96B_fdvsubgradmask_monai2dM_aux01det_frozen_philr01.yaml`; `tests/test_monai_unet_l96_2d.py`.
**Rationale:** Step 2 of the 2D-backbone study: DirectUNet-2D-M cut test RMSE by 27% (regular) / 14% (random layout) vs 1D DU-M; the hypothesis is that a local, sector-shared Phi makes x - Phi(x) more informative and widens the FDV2-vs-FDV1 gap. Parameter counts: FDV1-2D U 5.64 M (1D 5.89 M); FDV2-2D U 5.64 M + Phi 5.63 M (1D 5.90 + 5.88 M). Compute is ~9x the 1D convolution at matched parameters: 250 s/epoch (FDV1) and 485 s/epoch (FDV2) on an A100, vs 74 s/epoch for 1D FDV2-M.
**Verification:** `pytest tests/test_monai_unet_l96_2d.py` (block layout round trip; backbone and full FDV1/FDV2 solver exactly equivariant to cyclic sector shifts with NaN-masked observations; configs build with the expected counts; VanillaCFM-2D velocity equivariant, samples and loss finite); 1-epoch A100 smoke runs of both configs (val loss 0.57 / 0.45 after epoch 0).
