## 2026-10-12: Stochastic flow-matching posterior sampler (DPS / PiGDM, gamma, correctors, warm starts)

**Summary:** New `evaluation/fm_posterior_sampler.py` (`fm_posterior_sample`, `posterior_state_prediction`): samples the posterior of an unconditional FM prior (the SDA prior's velocity model) by guiding the Tweedie state prediction, `Psi_y = Psi + w c_tau grad log p(y|x_tau)` with DPS or PiGDM likelihoods (or the existing fixed-norm step), and integrating the posterior velocity with an Euler-Maruyama term `g(tau)^2 = 2 gamma sigma_p^2 (1-tau)/tau` plus optional Langevin correctors. Supports a warm start from one mean or from per-member starts, a per-window `tau0`, the early-fine grid, observation masks / NaNs / `obs_indices`, and per-channel `R_norm`; members run batched.
**Files modified:** `evaluation/fm_posterior_sampler.py` — new; `tests/test_fm_posterior_sampler.py` — new (15 fast CPU tests).
**Rationale:** Agent A1 of `docs/plans/analysis/l96_fdv2_fm_posterior.md` (PR #339): the FDV2->SDA hybrid is under-dispersed because its sampler is a deterministic ODE from a narrow warm start; this sampler re-injects noise at the rate the prior allows and uses a likelihood in normalised units, so R matters. Design notes: PiGDM defaults to the exact Tweedie covariance `c_tau J_Psi` (`pigdm_cov="tweedie"`), which makes `Psi_y` the exact Kalman update for a Gaussian prior; the plan's literal `r_tau^2 J_Psi` is `pigdm_cov="r2"`. The corrector step uses `eps = 2 snr^2 n / ||s||^2` (expected rather than drawn `||xi||^2`: the drawn one inflated the toy variance x2 in low dimension). `g = 0` at `tau = 0` (p_0 does not depend on y). With `gamma = 0`, `guidance="normalized"` the sampler reproduces `sda_guided_sample` (allclose, same seeds). All new behaviour is in a new module; nothing existing changes.
**Verification:** `pytest tests/test_fm_posterior_sampler.py tests/test_sda_sampler.py -m "not slow"` -> all pass; toy linear-Gaussian: stochastic PiGDM from noise and correctors from a mean warm start at tau0 = 0.7 match the analytic posterior mean/variance; the deterministic warm start at tau0 = 0.7 keeps < 20% of the posterior variance. `ruff check` clean.
